👑 King of the Stars
The High-Stakes Competitive Arena for Telegram Mini Apps.

King of the Stars is a social "King of the Hill" game where players spend Telegram Stars to seize a virtual throne. While holding the throne, players accumulate Honor Points to compete for a weekly prize pool distributed among the Top 10 earners.

Key Mechanics:

Real-time Throne Control: Instant takeovers and live event logs.
Dynamic Prize Economy: Automated 50% pool allocation.
Rarity Gear System: Mystery boxes with Common to Legendary items.
Internal Payout Loop: Sustainable "Bonus Stars" reward system.
